# Video Ad Concept & Prompts: Oidaq Guesthouse Management System

This document outlines the visual prompts, voiceover script, and production guidelines for an advertising video promoting **Oidaq**. The campaign is tailored for the **Botswana** and **South Africa** hospitality markets, focusing on the business benefits and peace of mind for guesthouse, B&B, and safari lodge owners.

---

## 1. Local Target Audience & Pain Points

### Target Markets
*   **Botswana (BW)**: Owner-operators of safari camps in Maun, delta lodges in Okavango, and city business guesthouses in Gaborone and Francistown.
*   **South Africa (ZA)**: Boutique B&Bs in Cape Town, business retreats in Johannesburg, and game lodges near Kruger National Park.

### Core Pain Points Addressed
1.  **The WhatsApp Black Hole**: Guest inquiries getting lost in personal WhatsApp threads.
2.  **Manual Feedback Chasing**: Spending hours chasing guests for reviews after they check out.
3.  **Wasted Marketing Budget**: Running social media ads blindly without knowing which ones are actually driving bookings.
4.  **High Middle-Man Fees**: Monthly subscription software and booking platforms taking a large cut of your revenue.

## 4. Audio & Music Guidelines
*   **Background Track**: Start with a slightly chaotic, fast acoustic guitar or marimba rhythm (representing the busy B&B environment). At the **0:10** mark (transition to Oidaq), drop the beat into a smooth, premium, uplifting corporate afro-house or acoustic track (combining modern tech synth with warm, local rhythmic undertones).
*   **Voiceover Tone**: A warm, reassuring, and professional South African or Motswana accent. The voice should feel like a trusted colleague offering a smart operational solution.
